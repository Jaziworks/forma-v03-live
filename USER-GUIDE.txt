FORMA — V03

Open V03.html in a current browser. All libraries, fonts and the logo are
embedded; the tool does not need an internet connection.

FONTS
Text > Font offers Monoline, Slanted, Sans outline and Serif outline.
Use "Import font from Files" to select a .ttf, .otf or .woff file from your
computer or the iPad Files app. Imported fonts are included in saved projects.
Installed device fonts cannot be automatically enumerated on iPad Safari.
WOFF2, color fonts and advanced contextual/RTL shaping are not supported.
Fonts produce outline strokes, not single-line centerlines. Use Monoline
for the original single-line style. Maximum 8 imported fonts, 8 MB per font.

TOUCH
In Move mode: one finger orbits; two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.
In Paint mode: draw with a finger or Apple Pencil; tap Move to navigate.
The Undo and Redo buttons are above the canvas.
On compact layouts, tap Layers to access composition, visibility and background.
Portrait layouts place scrollable controls below the canvas.
